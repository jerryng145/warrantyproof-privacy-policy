For assistance, email support@estreamedia.my with your app version, Android version and the inputs that caused the issue. Do not include sensitive personal information.

Inputs and units

Roof area is measured in square feet, rainfall in inches, storage in US gallons and daily use in gallons per day. Capture efficiency is a percentage. Enter the count and size of barrels already installed; stored water must fit within that capacity.

Calculation

Captured gallons = roof area × rainfall × 0.6233766234 × capture efficiency / 100. Target storage is stored water plus estimated capture. Existing capacity is barrel size × installed count. Each added-barrel option rounds up the shortfall divided by that option size. Resulting capacity keeps the existing barrels and adds the recommended barrels; headroom is capacity minus expected water. Coverage uses expected retained water divided by daily use. Zero daily use is shown as no estimate.

Saved plans and limits

Build 3 supports up to 100 named plans saved on this device. Create a plan, compare 55/100/custom additions, choose an option and Save. Open a saved plan to edit it; changes are saved only when you explicitly Save again. Delete asks for confirmation. Plans are not backed up or transferred; uninstalling or clearing storage removes them. Earlier build 2 was session-only. There are no accounts, automatic weather forecasts, live sensors, in-app purchases, or subscription features. This tool estimates quantities only. Confirm local harvesting, plumbing, contamination, mosquito-control and water-safety requirements independently.
