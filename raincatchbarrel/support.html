Inputs and units

Roof area is measured in square feet, rainfall in inches, storage in US gallons and daily use in gallons per day. Capture efficiency is a percentage. The barrel count is the number needed to hold the calculated total water, including water already stored.

Calculation

Captured gallons = roof area × rainfall × 0.6233766234 × capture efficiency / 100. Barrel count rounds up the total divided by your chosen barrel size. Days of water divides the calculated total by daily use. With zero daily use, the current app displays zero days.

Limits

Inputs reset when the app session ends. There are no saved plans, accounts, weather forecasts, live sensors, purchases, or subscription features. This tool estimates quantities only. Confirm local harvesting, plumbing, contamination, mosquito-control and water-safety requirements independently.
