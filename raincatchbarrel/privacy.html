RainCatch Barrel Planner Privacy

Publisher: E STREAM MEDIA EMPIRE. Effective date: October 5, 2026.

RainCatch Barrel Planner (Android package com.estreamedia.raincatchbarrel) is a free offline rainwater capacity calculator. It has no account, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, location access, or network permission.

Calculator inputs

Roof area, rainfall, efficiency, barrel size, stored water and daily use are processed on your device. The current version keeps inputs only during the current app session. It does not transmit them to us or store a personal profile.

Personal data

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data. It requests no sensitive runtime permissions. No cloud account, tracking SDK or remote calculator service is used.

Contact and privacy rights

If you email support@estreamedia.my, we receive the information you choose to include so we can respond. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents. You can ask us to delete that support correspondence. There is no in-app account or server record to delete.

Children and changes

The calculator has no registration or personal-data collection for any age group. This policy may be updated to reflect future app changes. The app is a planning aid and does not certify water safety or regulatory compliance.
