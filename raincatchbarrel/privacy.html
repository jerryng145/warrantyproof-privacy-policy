RainCatch Barrel Planner Privacy

Publisher: E STREAM MEDIA EMPIRE. Effective date: October 7, 2026.

RainCatch Barrel Planner (Android package com.estreamedia.raincatchbarrel) is a one-time paid download providing an offline rainwater catchment and barrel-storage planner. It has no account, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, location access, or network permission.

Store purchase

AppGallery handles the one-time paid download under its own privacy and payment policies. This app does not receive or collect your store payment details.

Local plans

Version 1.0.0 build 3 processes roof area, rainfall, efficiency, barrel size/count, stored water and daily use on your device. Explicit Save keeps your plan name, inputs, chosen barrel option and update time in private app storage so you can reopen it. Up to 100 plans can be saved. Plans are not transmitted to us or included in app cloud backup or device transfer. You can delete an individual plan in the app. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local plans. Earlier build 2 kept inputs only during its app session.

Personal data

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data. It requests no sensitive runtime permissions. No cloud account, tracking SDK or remote calculator service is used.

Contact and privacy rights

If you email support@estreamedia.my, we receive the information you choose to include so we can respond. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents. You can ask us to delete that support correspondence. There is no in-app account or server record to delete.

Children and changes

The planner has no registration or personal-data collection for any age group. This policy may be updated to reflect future app changes. The app is a planning aid and does not certify water safety or regulatory compliance.
